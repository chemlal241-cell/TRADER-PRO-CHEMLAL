# Module implementation status

- Risk Lab: risk/reward price-distance calculator and manual pre-trade checklist.
- Trade Journal: browser-local storage, table view, CSV export.
- Backtest Lab: statistics calculator for user-supplied R-multiple outcomes.

These modules do not fetch live XAUUSD market data, execute trades, sync journal records to a server, or perform candle-based historical backtests. Live prices, AI analysis, economic calendar/news, and validated multi-timeframe signals remain unimplemented.
